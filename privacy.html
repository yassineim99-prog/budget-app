Privacy Policy – Nisba

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

Nisba needs no account, has no analytics, and never sells your budget data. Without sharing, your budget never leaves your phone.

What the app stores and where

  • On your device: everything you enter (names, income, expenses, goals, settings) is saved in the app's local storage on your phone.
  • Optional sharing with invite codes: if you tap “Start sharing” or join with a code, your budget is stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase, Firestore) so the phones in your shared budget can sync. Each phone gets an anonymous ID; we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Only phones that joined with your invite code can read the shared budget. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). It is not used for advertising, analytics or anything else, and is never sold or shared.
  • Optional sync with your own Google script (advanced): your budget goes to a script in your own Google account, which the developer cannot access.
  • Backups you export are files you save and control.

Advertising

Nisba is free and may show ads, provided by Google (AdMob or AdSense). To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID or cookies, and may personalise ads where the law allows. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. Ads never see your budget: your amounts, categories and names are not shared with advertisers. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Permissions

The app uses internet access to load the app, to show ads, and, if you turn it on, to sync a shared budget. It does not access your contacts, location or microphone. The camera or photo library opens only when you tap Add a receipt photo; receipt photos are kept on your phone only and are never uploaded or shared.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything in the app, or uninstall it. If you share a budget, tap Settings → Stop sharing on this phone on each phone; when the last phone stops sharing, the shared copy is deleted from the cloud. If you used your own Google script, delete budget-app-data.json and the “Budget app backups” folder from your Google Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
